    Layout¶

    A widget's size and spacing are its own properties. Its parent places it.

    Overview¶

    Five groups of modules take part, with the Material components and the app at their edges.

    Structure¶

    flowchart LR
    sizing[The Sizing]
    containers[The Containers]
    providers[The Providers]
    composable[The Composable]
    fit[The Fit]
    window[The Window Sizing]
    material[the Material components]
    app[the app]

    app -- "width · height · padding · gap · alignment" --> containers
    app -- "builder(items, fn) · lazy(items, fn)" --> providers
    containers -- "provide_layout_children() · item_count() · set_window(start, stop, build)" --> providers
    containers -- "parse_sizing(value) · width_sizing · height_sizing · padding" --> sizing
    containers -- "width_sizing · height_sizing · layout_align · cross_align" --> composable
    material -- "width · length · size, per open axis" --> sizing
    app -- "fit · content_alignment" --> fit
    app -- "Window(width, height, position)" --> window
    window -- "the nine-point vocabulary" --> containers
    Module Role
    The Sizing how a widget asks for space on one axis
    The Containers place their children and share the room out
    The Providers build a container's children from data, all of them or the ones in view as the frame allows
    The Composable reports the layout metadata of the widget it built
    The Fit places a widget's content inside the rect it was given
    The Window Sizing the window's size and position, resolved before layout
    the Material components the MD3 widgets (MATERIAL_INTERACTION.md)
    the app sizes, spaces and aligns widgets

    Promises¶

    • padding and gap are the two spacing properties.
    • A modifier never changes layout (MODIFIER.md).
    • Alignment belongs to the parent.

    The Sizing¶

    A Sizing is how a widget asks for space on one axis.

    Structure¶

    flowchart LR
    sizing[rendering/sizing.py]
    kernel[widgeting/widget_kernel.py]
    row[layout/row.py · column.py]
    engine[layout/layout_engine.py]
    material[the Material components]

    kernel -- "parse_sizing(value) · sizing_signature(value)" --> sizing
    row -- "width_sizing · height_sizing: kind, value" --> kernel
    engine -- "sizing_signature(value), in the cache key" --> sizing
    row -- "preferred_size(widget, max_w, max_h)" --> measure
    measure[layout/measure.py]
    material -- "parse_sizing(length, default=Sizing.weight()) · style tokens for the fixed axis" --> sizing
    Module Role
    rendering/sizing.py Sizing, one of fixed, auto and weight, and its parser
    widgeting/widget_kernel.py holds a widget's two sizings (BOX_MODEL.md)
    layout/row.py · column.py share an axis out by the children's sizings (The Containers)
    layout/layout_engine.py keys its caches on the sizings (The Containers)
    layout/measure.py the measurement cache (The Containers)
    the Material components the fixed axes' style tokens

    Sequence¶

    A row shares its width out¶

    sequenceDiagram
    participant R as layout/row.py · column.py
    participant M as layout/measure.py
    participant C as the children

    R->>M: preferred_size(child, max_w, max_h), each child
    R->>R: the fixed and auto children take their size
    R->>R: the remainder, split by weight
    R->>C: set_layout_rect(x, y, w, h)

    Promises¶

    • Only the space left after the fixed and auto children is shared by weight.
    • A weight child measures as its content.
    • An axis MD3 leaves open is a constructor parameter, named width, length or size by its degree of freedom. An axis MD3 fixes is a style token.

    The Containers¶

    A container measures its children and allocates each a rect.

    Structure¶

    flowchart LR
    row[layout/row.py · column.py]
    grid[layout/grid.py]
    stack[layout/stack.py]
    container[layout/container.py]
    deck[layout/deck.py]
    flow[layout/flow.py · uniform_flow.py]
    alignment[layout/alignment.py]
    engine[layout/layout_engine.py]
    measure[layout/measure.py]
    utils[layout/layout_utils.py]
    children[the children]

    row & grid & stack & flow & container & deck -- "preferred_size(max_w, max_h) · set_layout_rect(x, y, w, h)" --> children
    row & grid & stack & flow & container & deck -- "preferred_size(widget, max_w, max_h), a child sized by its content" --> measure
    container -- "preferred_size · compute_inner_rect · compute_child_placement · resolve_child_geometry" --> engine
    row & grid & stack & container -- "normalize_alignment(value) · alignment_to_point(alignment, w, h)" --> alignment
    row & grid & stack & flow & container & deck -- "layout_child_if_needed(child, w, h, sized_by_parent) · paint_laid_out_children(...)" --> utils
    Module Role
    layout/row.py · column.py one axis shared out by sizing, main_alignment and cross_alignment across it, a gap between the children
    layout/grid.py rows, columns and areas; allocates each cell's rect
    layout/stack.py children over one another, each the sole claimant of both axes
    layout/container.py one child, placed by alignment
    layout/deck.py one page of several shown; every page laid out at the whole content box
    layout/flow.py · uniform_flow.py children wrapped into lines
    layout/alignment.py the nine-point vocabulary and its point in a rect
    layout/layout_engine.py the padding, border and placement arithmetic, cached (RENDERING_PIPELINE.md)
    layout/measure.py the measurement cache (RENDERING_PIPELINE.md)
    layout/layout_utils.py lays a child out, or leaves it pending past the frame's deadline; paints the children (RENDERING_PIPELINE.md)
    the children measured and placed

    Promises¶

    • Alignment positions a child. It never stretches one.
    • Whether a child fills its Grid cell is the child's own sizing.
    • Nothing clips a child larger than its rect.
    • A child sized by its parent on both axes, each fixed or weighted, is placed without being measured.
    • A pass short of time stops only at a child whose rect its parent decided: one sized by its parent on both axes, a Deck page, a Grid item in fixed tracks. The child keeps the rect and waits for idle time (RENDERING_PIPELINE.md).

    The Providers¶

    A provider builds a container's children from a data collection. A lazy container's provider builds the items in view, and the ones around them while the loop is idle. Inside a frame it builds until the layout deadline; placeholders hold the rest of the view until idle time.

    lazy[layout/lazy.py]
    containers[layout/row.py · column.py · uniform_flow.py]

    viewport[layout/scroll_viewport.py]
    controller[scrolling/controller.py]
    builder[widgeting/widget_builder.py]
    idle[widgeting/idle_work.py]

    containers -- "ForEach(items, fn, key_fn) · LazyItems(provider, axis, extent)" --> for_each & lazy
    containers -- "expand_layout_children(children)" --> utils
    utils -- "provide_layout_children()" --> for_each
    containers -- "count() · item_extent(container, cross_limit) · known_extent() · sample_size(cross_limit) · visible_span(container, length) · index_range(span, count, extent, gap, pad) · materialize(container, first, stop, pitch, place)" --> lazy
    lazy -- "place(child, index), for an item built one at a time · mark_needs_layout(), when the extent changed" --> containers
    viewport -- "preferred_size(max_w) · layout(w, h)" --> containers
    lazy -- "item_count() · set_window(start, stop, build) · window_children() · placeholder_indices() · build_item(index)" --> for_each
    lazy -- "visual_clip_rect() · visual_offset() · controller" --> viewport
    lazy -- "axis_state(axis).offset.subscribe(cb)" --> controller

    layout/lazy.py LazyItems: the item extent, measured on the first built item held in the container's layout pass when none is given, the span the viewport shows, the index range it covers, the held range around it and the lead; inside a frame builds until the layout deadline; follows the viewport's offset while mounted, builds ahead of it in idle time and keeps the items a view's length behind it

    scrolling/controller.py the offset Observable
    widgeting/widget_builder.py the recompose scopes (WIDGET_ARCHITECTURE.md)
    widgeting/idle_work.py the tasks the frame loop runs before it waits (RENDERING_PIPELINE.md)

    Sequence¶

    The first layout pass measures the item extent¶

    sequenceDiagram
    participant V as layout/scroll_viewport.py
    participant C as layout/column.py
    participant L as layout/lazy.py
    participant F as layout/for_each.py

    V->>C: preferred_size(max_w): the padding, no extent being known
    V->>C: layout(w, h)
    C->>L: item_extent(self, cross_limit)
    L->>F: set_window(0, 1), nothing being held: item 0 built
    L->>L: measure item 0, remember its extent
    L->>C: mark_needs_layout(): the measure caches of the container and its ancestors are dropped
    C->>L: materialize(self, first, stop, pitch, place), as in the case below
    V->>C: preferred_size(max_w), in the same pass: count × extent plus the gaps
    V->>C: layout(w, h) once more, at the settled length

    A scroll moves the window¶

    sequenceDiagram
    participant S as scrolling/controller.py
    participant L as layout/lazy.py
    participant C as layout/column.py
    participant V as layout/scroll_viewport.py
    participant F as layout/for_each.py

    S->>L: offset changed
    L->>L: note the direction and the items one step moves
    L->>C: mark_needs_layout()
    V->>C: preferred_size(max_w): count × extent plus the gaps
    V->>C: layout(w, h)
    C->>L: visible_span(self, h)
    L->>V: visual_clip_rect() · visual_offset()
    C->>L: index_range(span, count, extent, gap, pad)
    C->>L: materialize(self, first, stop, pitch, place)
    L->>L: the held range: the view, plus the built items within a view's length on each side
    L->>L: the layout deadline, when a frame is under way
    L->>F: set_window(held_first, held_stop, build)
    F->>F: build the items that entered the view, unmount the ones a view's length away. Inside a frame, a placeholder per item entered
    loop inside a frame, until the deadline
        L->>F: build_item(index): the view, from the scroll's side
        L->>C: place(child, index)
    end
    L->>I: request_idle_work(prefetch), when a placeholder remains or the lead is not full
    C->>C: place item i at pad + i × (extent + gap)

    Idle time builds ahead of the view¶

    sequenceDiagram
    participant E as backends/pyglet/event_loop.py
    participant I as widgeting/idle_work.py
    participant L as layout/lazy.py
    participant F as layout/for_each.py
    participant C as layout/column.py

    E->>I: run_idle_work(budget), before a wait
    I->>L: prefetch(deadline)
    loop until the deadline, or no placeholder remains and the lead is full
        alt a placeholder remains
            L->>F: build_item(index): the view first, then the nearest to it
            L->>C: place(child, index) · mark_needs_layout(), for an item in view
        else
            L->>L: the next item: the scroll's side first, at rest the side with fewer
            L->>F: set_window(the held range grown by one, build)
            F->>F: build the item
            L->>C: place(child, index)
        end
    end
    L-->>I: whether a placeholder remains or the lead is not full
    I-->>E: whether work remains

    Promises¶

    • A provider is never laid out or painted. Its children take its place in the container.
    • A lazy container holds a widget or a placeholder for each item in view, for the built items within a view's length on each side of it, and for none beyond. A placeholder takes the item's slot and no width.
    • An item that left the view keeps its widget until it lies a view's length away. The item comes back into view without a build.
    • The layout pass builds the items that entered the view and no other. Inside a frame it builds them one at a time, from the scroll's side, until the layout deadline; a placeholder holds each item left. Outside a frame, where no loop runs, it builds every item in view.
    • Idle time builds the placeholders first, the view before the rest, then the lead, one item at a time, the scroll's side first. A placeholder in view replaced asks the container for a layout.
    • At rest the lead is the same on both sides. While scrolling it grows with the items one scroll step moves.
    • Every item of a lazy container has one main-axis extent: the given one, else the extent of the first built item held, measured in the container's layout pass.
    • A lazy container's window is the span its nearest enclosing scroll viewport shows. With no viewport, the window is its own rect.
    • A lazy container's preferred_size builds no item. It answers the sum of its items and gaps once the extent is known, and the padding alone before the first layout pass.
    • A scroll viewport lays its content out at the length the content answers, reads the length again after the layout, and lays the content out once more when it changed.
    • A lazy container takes the cross-axis extent it is offered.

    The Composable¶

    A ComposableWidget reports the layout metadata of its built child.

    Structure¶

    flowchart LR
    widget[widgeting/widget.py]
    builder[widgeting/widget_builder.py]
    kernel[widgeting/widget_kernel.py]
    parent[the parent container]

    parent -- "width_sizing · height_sizing · layout_align · cross_align" --> widget

    Module Role
    widgeting/widget.py the declared flags and the getters
    widgeting/widget_builder.py the built child (WIDGET_ARCHITECTURE.md)
    widgeting/widget_kernel.py the stored values (The Sizing)

    Promises¶

    • A declared value wins. An undeclared one is derived from the built child.

    The Fit¶

    A leaf with content of its own places that content inside its rect by a fit mode.

    Promises¶

    • cover and fill exist for Image alone.

    The Window Sizing¶

    A window's size and position are resolved before layout.

    Structure¶

    flowchart LR
    sizing[runtime/window_sizing.py]
    window[runtime/window.py]
    placement[backends/pyglet/window_placement.py]
    runner[backends/pyglet/runner.py]
    alignment[layout/alignment.py]

    window -- "parse_window_sizing(value) · parse_window_position(value)" --> sizing
    window -- "the parsed WindowPosition" --> runner
    runner -- "apply_initial_position(pyglet_window, pos)" --> placement
    placement -- "the nine-point vocabulary" --> alignment
    Module Role
    runtime/window_sizing.py WindowSizing, fixed or auto; WindowPosition, an alignment on a screen plus an offset
    runtime/window.py resolves its size from the content's preferred size when auto (APP_WINDOW.md)
    backends/pyglet/window_placement.py places the outer frame in the screen's work area
    backends/pyglet/runner.py applies the position when the OS window is created (APP_WINDOW.md)
    layout/alignment.py the nine points (The Containers)

    Promises¶

    • A window position is resolved against the screen's work area, never its bounds. It places the outer frame, never the client area.
