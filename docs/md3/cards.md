# Cards MD3 Specs

Source: <https://m3.material.io/components/cards/specs>
Collected: 2026-05-25

## Summary

- Cards expose three active token sets in the live viewer: Card - Elevated, Card - Filled, and Card - Outlined.
- Elevated cards rest at 1dp on `surface-container-low`, rise to 3dp on hover, and jump to 8dp while dragged.
- Filled cards rest at 0dp on `surface-container-highest`, while outlined cards rest at 0dp on `surface` with a 1dp `outline-variant` border.
- All three variants share 12dp medium corner rounding, 24dp icon size, on-surface state-layer colors, and the same state-layer opacities: 0.08 hover, 0.1 focus, 0.1 pressed, and 0.16 dragged.
- The live viewer still exposes deprecated `surface-tint-layer` tokens for elevated, filled, and outlined cards; new implementations should use the resolved container roles directly.

## Implementation Notes

- Use `md.sys.shape.corner.medium` as a real 12dp corner radius across elevated, filled, and outlined cards.
- Normalize all level-0 card elevations to 0dp in implementation even though the raw payload can serialize them without a numeric component.
- Elevated cards use a stronger elevation ladder than filled and outlined cards: 1dp resting, 3dp hover, and 8dp dragged.
- Filled and outlined cards share the same interaction-state layer colors and opacities; outlined cards additionally promote the outline color to `md.sys.color.on-surface` on focus.
- Ignore the deprecated `surface-tint-layer` rows for new work; the resolved container color tokens are the implementation-relevant values.
- Color values in the tables were taken from the rendered viewer rows because this page's payload-level resolved colors do not match the visible token viewer output.
